# Islamic source policy

Only edition-specific, rights-cleared and qualified-review-approved Quran, Hadith, translations, Tafsir and Dua may be published. Record source authority, edition, canonical identifier, original text hash, translator, grading authority where relevant, license, review decision and manifest version. Keep canonical text immutable; normalization/search indexes are derived. Never generate Quran text or Hadith attribution from model memory. Distinguish translation, commentary and Madhhab differences. Use synthetic labeled fixtures until actual rights and review evidence exists. Any integrity mismatch blocks publication; corrections require reviewed new version and rollback plan. AI must retrieve and cite approved sources or abstain. See CONTENT_LICENSE_REGISTRY.md and SCHOLAR_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md.
